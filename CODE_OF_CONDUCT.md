# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, give technical criticism without personal attacks, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, community channels, events, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact address published on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/usernamenenad). Do not include sensitive reports in a public issue.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, review them as confidentially as practical, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a private warning, content removal, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Anyone handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when necessary.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this code.
